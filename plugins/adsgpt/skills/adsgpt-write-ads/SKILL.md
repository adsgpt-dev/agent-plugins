# Write ads

The copy of the ads of one campaign plan, for the formats its platforms take.

- **Language.** Write in the language of the campaign's audience, or in the language of the plan
  when the audience has none. Capitalize as that language does: in Spanish and Portuguese, only the
  first word and proper nouns («Monetiza tu app con Wortise», not «Monetiza Tu App Con Wortise»).
  Title case is an English convention.
- **One idea per creative.** Each creative is a different idea: a different angle on why the
  audience should act, such as the outcome, a proof, the price, the speed or a risk it avoids, not
  the same message reworded. Name the idea in one sentence.
- **The real brand.** Name the real brand and product: the brand of the plan, or the one of its
  landing page. What its site states says what it offers. Invent no prices, offers, discounts,
  awards or claims that the plan or the site do not state.
- **What people search.** The keywords are what people search, and what the ads answer: write
  about that, for the people who search it, with their words.
- **Specific, not generic.** Lead with the offer or the outcome, not the company's name. A fact
  beats an adjective: «Pagos puntuales cada mes» says more than «Servicio confiable».
- **The user's brief.** Follow it when there is one: it is what they asked of the ads.
- **Combinable texts.** Responsive ads, such as search, app, Performance Max, Demand Gen and display
  ads, combine headlines and descriptions, so each one must read well on its own and next to any
  other. Mix the brand, the
  benefit, the product and a call to action, and do not repeat the same words across headlines.
- **Exact limits.** Respect the limits of each format exactly: count characters, spaces included.
  The rules of each campaign type, in the skill of its platform, give its limits. Write the most
  headlines the format takes: they give the platform more to combine.
- **Refreshing ads that run.** Start from evidence: the searches that brought clicks and the ads
  with the best click-through rate, from the platform's reports. Keep the ad that works running
  while a new one gathers data.
